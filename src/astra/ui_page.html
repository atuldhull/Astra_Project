What live checking catches: a lost fix or a sudden drop in satellites while flying (jamming-like), and a position that reappears impossibly far away after an outage (spoofing-like). A spoof that keeps the receiver's fix healthy, like the spoof-jump example, needs a Shield calibration made from at least half an hour of this drone's normal flights, or a log check after landing. On real field attacks the receiver checks raised the alarm 3 times out of 3, within 10–20 s.
