# A6 v2: EKF tuning advice with realistic GNSS noise (ArduPilot + PX4)

Same attacks as A6 v1: 24 slow drag-offs, 0.5–1 m/s, half naive and half consistent. Benign cost was measured
on 20 flights with `typical` GNSS (median real receiver) and with `harsh` GNSS (noisiest ~10% of real
receivers, `gnss-realism-v1.md`). "Alerts" are operator-visible navigation messages (e.g. EKF lane switch).

## ArduPilot (EKF3)

| setting | naive caught | consistent caught | benign alerts, typical GNSS | benign alerts, harsh GNSS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| default | 0/12 | 0/12 | 0/20 | 0/19 |
| tight-gates (300) | 0/12 | 0/12 | 0/20 | 0/19 |
| tighter-gates (200) | 6/12 | 0/12 | 0/20 | **3/20** |
| low-noise | 6/12 | 2/12 | 0/20 | 1/20 |
| tight+low-noise+glitch 10 | **12/12** | 3/12 | 2/20 | **14/16**, plus 3 flights that never took off |

## PX4 (EKF2)

| setting | naive caught | consistent caught | benign alerts, typical | benign alerts, harsh |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| default | 0/12 | 0/12 | 0/20 | 0/20 |
| tight-gates (3σ) | 5/12 | 0/12 | 0/20 | 0/20 |
| low-noise (P 0.3, V 0.15) | 0/12 | 0/12 | 0/18 | 0/20 |
| tight+low-noise | 6/12 | 1/12 | 0/20 | 0/20 |

## Reading

- **The A6 v1 headline doesn't survive realistic GNSS.** ArduPilot `tight+low-noise` catches 12/12 naive drags,
  but it alerts on 14 of 16 benign flights with a noisy receiver (EKF3 lane switches), and 3 more flights
  never took off. It is safe only on airframes with a known-good receiver. Check it on the vendor's own logs
  first.
- **ArduPilot `low-noise`** (6/12 naive, 1/20 harsh alerts) and **PX4 `tight-gates`** (5/12 naive, 0/20 harsh)
  are the best trade-offs found. Both are partial: tuning roughly halves the naive gap.
- **Consistent drags stay at ≤ 3/12 with every setting.** Tuning cannot fix them (05-DETECTION-PHYSICS §1).
- **80-flight confirmation (2026-11-12, `a6-confirm-ardupilot.md`, `a6-confirm-px4.md`):** PX4 `tight-gates`
  holds (0/78 alerts vs stock 0/76; 95% upper bound ~3.8%). ArduPilot `low-noise` does **not**: 7/79 alerts
  (stock 0/75). The 1/20 above was luck of a small sample. For ArduPilot with an average receiver, no tested
  setting is recommended; the advisor should be run on the vendor's own receiver noise.
- Screening samples above are small (16–20 benign flights each). Use `astra advise --gps-profile harsh --nbenign 80` before
  changing a fielded airframe.
